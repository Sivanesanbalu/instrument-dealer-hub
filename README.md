## 🌟 Core Features

- **65+ Verified Indian OEMs & Stockists:** Covers Pressure, Temperature (RTD), Flowmeters, Level Transmitters, Control Valves, Gas Detectors, and Metrology Instruments across Tamil Nadu, Maharashtra, Gujarat, Karnataka, Delhi NCR, and Pan-India.
- **🛡️ Anti-Fraud Due Diligence Audit:** Direct Government GSTIN verification (`services.gst.gov.in`), physical Google Maps factory inspection, and anti-fraud bank transfer safety protocols.
- **🌐 Live Internet Crawler:** Scans DuckDuckGo & industrial directories for real-time live suppliers.
- **📲 1-Click WhatsApp & Gmail RFQ Broadcaster:** Pre-filled technical RFQs with Target Buying Price, Quantities, Specs, and online quotation portal links.
- **💰 Reseller Margin Calculator & Client Quotation Generator:** Instantly calculates profit margins + 18% GST and prints formal commercial quotations for your buyers.
- **📥 Quote Comparison & CSV Export:** Compare dealer bids and export comparison sheets to Excel.
